# Additional AH mounts, Classic through Midnight

Reviewed 2026-10-21 for Retail. Added **20 mount items**, **33 map choices** and **34 labelled pins** to Drops > Mounts. The curated catalog grows from 7 to **27 mounts**, with 45 map choices and 113 pins overall. The original seven row identities and their map options are retained.

This is an expansion-by-expansion discovery pass, not an exhaustive mount database. Additions must have a gameplay acquisition path and a saleable finished mount item. TCG redemptions, shop purchases, BMAH purchases, bound outputs, and tradeable recipes for bound mounts are not added as farmable mount output. No AH prices, margins, drop rates or hourly yields are promised.

## Binding and map decisions

- All 20 additions have successfully retrieved current Retail item tooltips, acquisition sources and tradeability corroboration. The source-index JSON retains retrieval timestamps and SHA-256 receipts. The raw cache is under `.local/ah-mount-sources/`. A learned mount cannot be caged; guides explicitly tell users to keep the sale item unlearned.
- [Void-Crystal Panther](https://www.wowhead.com/item=235712) binds to Warband despite an older tradeability listing. [Rope Lynx Harness](https://www.wowhead.com/item=270058/rope-lynx-harness) binds on pickup; [its pattern](https://www.wowhead.com/item=272391/pattern-rope-lynx-harness) is the tradeable item. Both mount outputs are excluded.
- Outland machines and Flying Carpet retain current expansion-specific rider skill requirements. The two motorcycles retain faction and reputation requirements. Panther designs retain their individual reputation ranks, and the Onyx recipe consumes the four colored mounts. The two rockets retain their Engineering specializations.
- Sky Golem documents the 30 daily Jard crafts and 30 Living Steel. Its Granary pin is a reported recipe-farming area, not a guaranteed drop. Vial of the Sands uses a named supplier pin; random Archaeology digsites are not fabricated as permanent recipe locations.
- Maps use Retail UI-map IDs and normalized coordinates. Roles distinguish recipe trainers, suppliers, dungeon/raid entrances, quest areas and access anchors. Separate faction entrances remain separate dropdown choices. Every point uses `farm-mount` and `isRoute = false`; there are no fabricated route lines or surface boss spawns.
- Garrison layout varies by tier. Coalfist markers are explicitly outdoor access anchors; its instructions direct users to the Town Hall follower table and random Breaker Two offer. Grumpus includes the seasonal daily area, an Alliance Izzy supplier marker and a Horde garrison access marker; the enemy itself is not described as the mount drop.
- Big Keech is a documented original-Vale patrol anchor, not a guaranteed stationary vendor. Conflicting recent availability reports and phase instructions are retained in the guide. Jonathan Garrett uses the documented approximate stables area (29, 28), without invented decimal precision.
- Paxton Bombshell uses the post-Undermine Ringing Deeps position 59.76, 78.43. The older 63.8 marker is not used. Vendor components appear only in acquisition instructions; no vendor reagent rows are introduced.
- Corrected the existing Garn Nighthowl guide that incorrectly described the saleable item as binding on pickup.
