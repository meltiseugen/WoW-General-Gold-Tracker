# Additional AH pets, Classic through Midnight

Reviewed 2026-10-21 for Retail. Added **29 species**, each with its own Drops > Pets row and researched farm, **29 map options** and **44 labelled pins**. The curated catalog grows from 153 to 182 pet rows. Existing raid pets and migrated Pandaria research are retained.

This is an expansion-by-expansion discovery pass, not a claim that every saleable pet has been catalogued. No current AH prices, sale rates, farming yields or live WoW results are asserted. Source pages can contain obsolete Classic skill requirements or old drop estimates; those numbers were not imported as Retail guarantees.

## Saleability and map decisions

- Species IDs identify pets in pricing and farm indexes. Item 82800 is only the shared cage representation; it never becomes the lookup key for an individual pet.
- Green Wing Macaw, Elementium Geode, Curious Lynx Kitten and Wriggling Capybara permit sale of the original unlearned item. Other new entries instruct the player to learn the bound/direct reward and cage the pet before auctioning it. In particular, Giant Sewer Rat's original item is BoP. The bound Midnight event pouch and its tradeable pet contents have different binding rules.
- Midnight's Dali and Niblet, Dragonflight's Vorquin Runt, and TWW's Marmaduke and Mossy Snail were rejected because the retrieved database entries mark them uncageable. Existing catalog pets were not duplicated.
- Dragonhawk Munchkin is explicitly a Renown 12 currency-vendor option costing 2,500 Voidlight Marl. It is not described as a random drop.
- Proper Parrot uses species 2541 from formerly unused BFA data, but is grouped with its obtainable Siren Isle source in The War Within.
- Map IDs are Retail UI-map IDs, not area IDs: Dalaran Underbelly 126, assault Uldum 1527, assault Vale 1530, Midnight Eversong 2395 and Midnight Zul'Aman 2437.
- Deadmines uses the outdoor mine approach and instructions to continue to the portal. Phoenix Hatchling uses the original Magisters' Terrace entrance. Neither fabricates an interior boss pin.
- Elementium and southeastern Dinomancer ship pins are labelled farming-area anchors. Skikx'traz is labelled a flying-patrol search anchor. Void Strike pins are documented rotating event anchors, with no claim of a permanent pet or chest spawn.
- Lost Quill includes the ink and upper-wall quill separately. Roseate Hopper includes its final location and all three ingredient locations. Proper Parrot retains seven named pirate elite positions and both rare positions; Crackleroar includes three storm-phase farming areas.
- Every new map option has source URLs, confidence, map role, labelled normalized coordinates and `isRoute = false`. Rendered pins use the established `farm-pet` type. No route lines are invented between separate acquisition points.

## Validation

The focused embedded Lua 5.1 run passed **32 specs**, covering the pet catalog, actual Drops row construction, every new Map button, exact species-to-farm resolution, map-option conversion, and catalog exclusion checks. It is not a native Busted run. Five edited/new Lua files parsed successfully. Manifest validation passed for six manifests and 239 runtime Lua files.

Five additional lazy-loader specs passed, including real manifest-driven loading of the Farms module and its new research file. The local Farms addon is already a junction to this checkout, so no deployment copy was necessary.

Broader farm-detail and catalog-loader specs produced 40 passes and four failures, with the identical result when the new pet additions were disabled in memory. Three existing assertions expect older valuation-text wording (`farm_details_window_spec.lua`, lines 665, 710 and 769); one expects a startup price-candidate count between 1,000 and 1,500 (`catalog_loader_spec.lua`, line 146). These unrelated failures were not changed as part of the pet additions.

Live WoW `/reload`, native map rendering and AH listings were not tested. Source positions, phases, currency costs and cageability remain subject to game updates. The existing Drops pet catalog remains always visible rather than taking generic scan filters.
