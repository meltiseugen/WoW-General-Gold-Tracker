# Draenor specialty farms and crafted items

Researched 2026-10-19 for Retail. This pass targets **Warlords of Draenor**. The earlier Pandaria additions are retained.

The catalog adds **344 entries**: 334 tradeable products and 10 required bound support inputs. Of these, **267 have composed recipes**: 258 tradeable outputs and nine bound intermediates. There are **22 housing decor recipes**, **32 individually identified random Darkmoon cards**, and **45 additional gathering, processing, treasure, mission or support records**. The complete Draenor catalog now contains 390 entries. Existing Primal Spirit is also explicitly marked bound, bringing the expansion's bound support total to 11.

All additions have `isRoute = false`. The existing herb and ore route modules retain their coordinates, options and route flags. No raw ore/herb entries or vendor-only supplies were added. Existing ore/herb items remain valid ingredients of composed profession recipes.

## Discovery and recipe verification

- [AllTheThings current Draenor crafted-item index](https://github.com/DFortun81/AllTheThings/blob/master/.contrib/.db/standard/09%20-%20Crafted%20Items/06%20-%20Warlords%20of%20Draenor.lua) supplies profession/output discovery and removed-item annotations. Its reagent cost entries are **not output batch sizes**.
- Ackis profession recipe lists provide additional Draenor spell candidates. Current Retail Wowhead item/spell tooltips verify binding, quantities, recipe sources, cooldowns and output batches. Each selected recipe has its own item/spell URL in the companion JSON and loaded research record.
- [Blizzard's profession decor announcement](https://worldofwarcraft.blizzard.com/en-us/news/24247147/professions-unpacked-craft-housing-decor-with-reagents-from-every-era) establishes the old-profession housing use. Quantities come from current per-recipe tooltips, rather than older announcement or PTR totals. The current [Beloved Murloc Plushie recipe](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=1315766) brings this pass to 22 decor outputs.
- The [machine-readable research archive](draenor-specialty-2026-10-19.json) records all selected compositions, full ingredient lists, omitted vendor supplies, alternative/discarded candidates, random-card attempt cost and exclusions.

Coverage includes Alchemy flasks/potions/oils and the Stone of Fire chain; Blacksmithing armor/weapons; Cooking meals/feasts and Fel Eggs and Ham; Enchanting scrolls and the Draenor illusion tome; Engineering gadgets, pets, scopes and fireworks; Inscription inks, weapons, cards, decks and trinkets; Jewelcrafting jewelry/gems; Leatherworking armor, bags, cloaks and drums; Tailoring armor, cloaks, bandages, bags and decor.

## Binding, output and exclusions

Current Alchemical Catalyst is tradeable. Hexweave Cloth, Burnished Leather, Truesteel Ingot and Taladite Crystal bind on pickup; their chosen daily recipes guarantee at least eight, with skill-dependent additional output. Gearspring Parts and War Paints use a four-output minimum. Daily cooldowns and variable yields are labeled. Draenor Cooking tooltips state a four-serving baseline for the relevant recipes; actual Cooking yield varies. Card of Omens produces ten per craft. Greater flasks, gliders and fireworks retain their actual batch sizes.

Darkmoon Card of Draenor's placeholder item is excluded. Its 32 real card outcomes have `randomOutput = true` and no invented deterministic cost per individual card. Matching decks require the eight individual cards. A completed Draenor deck opens directly into its matching trinket, without a Faire quest hand-in. Full random-attempt ingredients are archived separately.

Bound finished items such as the original Elekk Plushie, Creeping Carpet and Blingtron 5000 are excluded as AH products. Bound intermediates needed by saleable crafts are retained with `auctionable = false`; this includes the Stone of Wind/Earth/Waters chain. Old removed upgrade essences, obsolete Rapid Mind elixir, research placeholders and unverified shipment placeholders are excluded. Recipe patterns being sold by a vendor does not disqualify an otherwise craftable sale product.

Vendor-only Crystal Vial, Simple Wood, Light Parchment, Enchanting Vellum, Curing Salt, Perfect Preservatives, Gorgrond Grapes and Telmor-Aruuna Hard Cheese are omitted from new component lists. Recipe labels note omitted supplies where applicable. Enchant scroll labels explain applying the enchant to vellum. Consequently composed costs describe the farmable portion, not every purchased expense.

- Addon manifest validation passes: six manifests, 216 runtime Lua files. Changed task files have no whitespace errors; the repository-wide whitespace check still reports an existing trailing blank line in BACKLOG.md.
- Preservation comparison verifies non-Draenor catalog rows/index entries and supplied Draenor herb/ore module contents are unchanged from this pass's starting snapshots.
- No live WoW runtime or Auction House test was performed. Sources establish acquisition and binding, not present-day demand, profit, measured yields or guaranteed rare mission offers.
