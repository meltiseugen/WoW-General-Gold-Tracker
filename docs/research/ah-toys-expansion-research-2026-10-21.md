# Additional AH toys, Classic through Midnight

Reviewed 2026-10-21 for Retail. Added **28 toys**, **47 map choices** and **62 labelled pins** to Drops > Toys, with at least one addition from every expansion era. The curated catalog grows from 8 to **36 toys**, with **55 map choices and 78 pins** overall. Original row IDs and the existing eight map choices / sixteen pins are retained.

This is a discovery pass, not a complete catalog of every saleable toy. Only verified Toy Box outputs with a gameplay acquisition path are added. Non-toy gadgets, consumables, bound rewards, shop/TCG redemption products and Darkmoon activities are not added. A tradeable recipe does not by itself make its output saleable. No AH prices, sale rates, profit margins or hourly yields are promised.

## Map and acquisition decisions

- All 28 additions have current Retail tooltip receipts confirming their Toy classification and no pickup/Warband binding restriction. The source index stores retrieval timestamps and SHA-256 hashes; raw responses are cached under `.local/ah-toy-sources/`. Every guide tells the player to keep the finished item unlearned for sale.
- The curated `ToyFarmData.rows` combines the eight imported rows with the 28 additions. The generated raw Drops snapshot is retained. Toys remain always visible, and Map resolves the exact source record rather than a collateral farm for the same item.
- Pins use normalized coordinates on Retail UI maps. Roles distinguish recipe sources, entrance/access points, crafting stations, quest steps, farming areas and optional upgrades. Every rendered pin carries the toy name, action, tips and source URLs, uses `farm-toy` and has `isRoute = false`.
- Orb of Deception is a very rare world drop; Blackwing Lair/Nefarian is a documented source supported by [Retail player reports](https://us.forums.blizzard.com/en/wow/t/orb-of-deception/371254) and [WoWDB source commentary](https://www.wowdb.com/items/1973-orb-of-deception). The guide claims no guaranteed drop or measured best farm, and preserves the weekly raid limit. The pin is an entrance/access marker. Old Dire Maul arena rare advice is not imported as a current spawn farm.
- World Enlarger marks the original Blackrock Depths entrance and identifies Weapon Technicians in the Manufactory as schematic sources. The entrance coordinate is reused from the existing Retail DB2-derived instance research (`InstanceLocationOverrides.lua`, build 12.1.5.70077); this is not a fresh live map measurement. Crashin' Thrashin' Robot likewise marks an eligible schematic-source raid entrance, not a finished-toy boss drop.
- The two Classic transporters and two Outland transporters retain Engineering specializations and dialogue-based recipe learning. Everlook uses the post-Cataclysm map coordinate 59.7,49.8 from documented comments, rather than the obsolete 61,38 location. Northrend crafts point to map 125; Legion crafts point to map 627.
- Cataclysm recipes include both faction capital trainers. Loot-A-Rang requires skill 40 to craft but skill 70 to use; Gnomish Gravity Well requires the Gnomish specialization. World Shrinker follows its current tooltip, without copying the older wiki's extra Gnomish-use restriction.
- Draenor recipes identify the one-Secret unlock and daily currency/component systems. Nik Steelrings has a documented Warspear position. The Alliance marker is explicitly a Lunarfall access anchor; players must find Zaren Hoffle inside their own Engineering Works, whose placement varies by garrison layout. Sean Catchpole in Stormshield is also named as an alternative without inventing his exact pin.
- Coin of Many Faces marks the documented Isle of Shadows skeleton farm at 40,83. Hallow's End calendar restrictions and the random enemy drop are explicit; the coin is not described as a guaranteed daily-quest reward.
- Blingtron's tutorial includes Didi and every Short Circuit stop in order: Rocket-Chief Fuselage, Ricket, Chief Engineer Foote, Nixx, Winkey and Gnopetto's underwater workshop. The guide retains the earlier Legion profession quest/dungeon prerequisites and borrowing the diving helmet.
- Argus includes all five recorder survey areas plus Didi's start/turn-in. Coordinates are labelled survey areas because documented comments differ slightly; use active quest markers for interaction range. Eredath is explicitly linked to its former Mac'Aree name.
- Both Nazjatar faction trainers remain separate dropdown choices and teach both BFA generators. Shadowlands uses Dar Vattish on Oribos's main Ring of Fates level, with Honored Undying Army and skill 70 recipe requirements; the misleading trainer-only wiki summary is not followed.
- Dragonflight maps identify the Engineering station area and specialization unlocks. The Wyrmhole map additionally includes all 15 optional Signal Transmitters across five zones and the optional Zaralek compass. These are buyer destination upgrades, separate from crafting the sale item; no sale listing is described as unlocking them.
- Khaz Algar uses the Inventing/prototype/Scour system and a labelled Dornogal crafting area. Midnight uses Recycling discovery, its skill-tree gate and current Silvermoon map 2393 at Danwe's Bazaar Engineering area. Neither marker represents a guaranteed recipe vendor or toy drop. Minimum use levels are retained where present in current tooltips.
