Import a route in under a minute

Every entry is formatted for the Routes AddOn. Keep Routes enabled, choose the loop you need and import it directly in game.

Required: Routes must be installed and enabled before importing a string.

1

Copy the route

Choose an expansion, find the zone or resource and use its Copy button.

2

Open Routes

In World of Warcraft, open the Routes AddOn and go to Import/Export.

3

Paste and save

Paste the copied string, confirm the import and activate the route you want to follow.
