    Midnight Majestic Beast Lure Locations

    Written by Roguery - 28th February 2026

    The Majestic Beasts are an integral part of farming skinning materials because they have a chance to drop the Majestic reagents (Majestic Fin, Majestic Claw and Majestic Hide) which are used in a lot of higher end crafting recipes.

    One of the harder parts of farming these materials is knowing where to place the lures after you have crafted them. Here we have the locations you can place all of the beast lures in the Midnight expansion.

    Majestic Beast Lure Locations

    Each Zone has its own Beast Lure and a special Grand Beast lure all of which have to be placed in a specific location. These can be VERY hard to find as the only indicator you receive is a debuff Sixth Sense, oddly this debuff does not show up when you are mounted.

    Majestic Eversong Lure Location

    The Majestic Eversong Lure summons Gloomclaw, a large cat. The lure location is in the South of Eversong Woods a little east of Windrunner Spire.

    /way #2395 41.94 79.71

    Majestic Zul’Aman Lure Location

    The Majestic Zul’Aman Lure summons Silverscale, a turtle. The lure location is right on the east of Zul’Aman under the large bridge.

    /way #2437 47.56 52.63

    Majestic Harandar Lure Location

    The Majestic Harandar Lure summons Lumenfin, a sporeglider. The lure location is in the east of Harandar just outside Har’athir.

    /way #2413 66.61 47.84

    Majestic Voidstorm Lure Location

    The Majestic Harnardar Lure summons Umbrafang, a shredclaw. The lure location is in a ravine a little north of the main hub in the voidstorm, The Howling Ridge.

    /way #2405 54.12 65.23

    Grand Beast Lure Location

    The Grand Beast Lure summons Netherscythe, a large flying creature. The lure location is a little south of Locus Point in the Voidstorm.

    /way #2405 43.24, 82.83

    The lures are the only way to receive some of the rarest materials, but finding their locations can be frustrating. We hope these locations helped you. If you are looking for more information on the skinning profession check out our Midnight Skinning Profession Guide.
