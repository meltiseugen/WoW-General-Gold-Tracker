# General Gold Tracker & Farming Helper

**Find your next farm. Track every drop. See what your time is worth.**

General Gold Tracker brings session tracking, farming research, maps, and market information together in one Retail WoW addon. Whether you're gathering herbs, running old raids, hunting rare drops, or building a farming routine across your alts, GGT helps you choose a target and keep track of the results.

Start a session, head out into the world, and watch your looted gold, estimated Auction House value, vendor value, and gold per hour build as you play.

## Know what your farming session is worth

Keep the numbers you care about close at hand:

- **Live session totals:** Track raw gold, estimated AH item value, and vendor value with separate hourly rates.
- **Loot highlights:** Pick a value threshold and make your best drops stand out.
- **Flexible tracking:** Start and stop manually, or choose automatic session starts in Options.
- **Three window sizes:** Use the full tracker, a compact summary, or a tiny total-only view.
- **Session history:** Revisit item breakdowns, dates, locations, and the character behind each run. Merge sessions or split merged runs by location.

Compare your own results over time and decide which farms deserve another visit.

## Find something worth farming

Open **Explorer** to browse farming opportunities and keep your research in one place.

- **Materials:** Explore profession materials and farming inputs by expansion and profession.
- **Instances:** Browse dungeon and raid drops, including auctionable gear and transmog targets.
- **Rares:** Look through rare-drop opportunities and filter by your chosen item value.
- **Drops:** Browse mounts, pets, toys, recipes, and cosmetics, alongside eligible BoE loot recorded from your own sessions.
- **Favorites:** Save interesting finds so they're easy to revisit.

Use filters, sortable columns, and saved scans to narrow a long list down to your next target. Where available, item and farm details include locations, routes, tips, and source links.

## Put your plans on the map

Open available farming maps to see locations, route overlays, and recorded loot coordinates. Compare location choices and read the accompanying farming guidance before setting off.

Build **custom farms**, collect your targets into a plan, assign farms to characters, and arrange route stops. Export a farm or your whole collection as a shareable string, or import someone else's setup.

## Follow the market

With **TradeSkillMaster**, GGT can value your loot using your preferred price source and an ordered fallback list for missing prices.

- **Market Watch:** Spot material price increases and decreases against previous saved snapshots.
- **Item price history:** Review locally recorded price observations as your history builds.
- **Bags:** Check the estimated auction value of eligible items already in your inventory.
- **Optional realm comparisons:** Compare supported non-commodity item prices across realms downloaded by your TSM desktop app.

**AH values are estimates, not completed sales.** Your selected price source and available TSM data determine the values shown.

## Keep your alts in view

The **Alts** tab brings recorded character locations, professions, and farm assignments together. Select a character to explore its saved location on an interactive map: drag to pan, scroll to zoom, and use **Reset view** to return to the starting view.

Character locations are saved snapshots captured around login and logout.

## Make it yours

Choose from six bundled visual themes: **Midnight Classic, Ember Parchment, Quiet Botanical, Quiet Arcane, Field Ledger,** and **Minimal Voidglass**.

Adjust item quality filters, loot highlights, notifications, pricing sources, session behavior, and history settings. Favorites, saved scans, farms, and session history are shared across characters on the same WoW account.

## Getting started

1. Install General Gold Tracker and keep all bundled addon folders together in your AddOns directory.
2. Type `/gt start` and begin farming.
3. Type `/gt` to open the tracker, or `/gt explorer` to browse farming opportunities.
4. Type `/gt stop` when you're finished, then review your session history.

Use `/gt options` to customize the addon and `/gt help` for the command list.

**Supported game:** World of Warcraft Retail.

**TradeSkillMaster is optional.** Raw gold and vendor-value tracking work without it; TSM supplies the Auction House pricing features. For optional realm comparisons, configure the additional realms in the TSM desktop app and enable the bundled TSM Realm Prices module.
